About

I'm a multimedia content creator and visual storyteller with a background in photography, videography, and post-production editing. I've worked as a photographer for The Walt Disney Company and run Southivong Productions, where I create photo and video content for brands, averaging 90K+ monthly views across social platforms.

I hold a B.S. in Film and Digital Media from the University of North Georgia. I shoot on both Sony and Canon systems, with hands-on set experience from student films, and I'm fluent in Adobe Suite for editing along with Google Suite for workflow and project management.

This site is a growing collection of my favorite work and the gear I use to make it.
