The Kit

Six pieces, zero excuses
01

Sony A7 IV

My workhorse body for everything from trackside panning shots to low-light wildlife. Full-frame sensor, fast autofocus, and in-body stabilization I lean on constantly for handheld video.

02

Fujifilm X100VI

The camera that never leaves the bag. Compact enough for street and travel days when a full kit isn't practical, with color science I don't get anywhere else.

03

Sony FE 70–200mm f/2.8 GM II

The lens behind almost every motorsport shot on this site. Fast enough to freeze cars at speed, with the compression that makes a corner exit look dramatic.

04

Sony FE 24–105mm f/4 G

My do-everything zoom. It's on the camera for landscapes, events, and anything in between where switching lenses would cost me the shot.

05

Rode VideoMic Pro

Clean, directional audio for run-and-gun video work, no separate recorder required. Rides on the hot shoe and stays out of the way.

06

DJI Ronin RS 3 Pro

Turns handheld footage into something that looks tripod-smooth, even walking trackside or moving through a crowd.

Multimedia Storyteller

Chasing motion, wildlife & faces in the crowd.

I'm a multimedia content creator and visual storyteller who built a career out of chasing the moment right before it disappears — a car mid-corner, an animal mid-glance, a sky mid-change. What started as a hobby with a hand-me-down camera turned into a full creative practice spanning photography, videography, and post-production editing.

I've worked as a PhotoPass photographer for The Walt Disney Company, capturing thousands of guest portraits a day in a fast-paced, high-volume environment — the work that shaped my eye for portraiture and directing people in front of the camera. I also run Southivong Productions, where I develop photo and video content for brands that consistently pulls in 90K+ monthly views across social platforms.

I hold a B.S. in Film and Digital Media from the University of North Georgia, where I built the technical foundation — camera operation, lighting, and post workflow — that shows up in every frame I deliver today. I shoot across both Sony and Canon systems depending on the job, with hands-on set experience from student films, and I'm fluent in the Adobe and Google Suites for everything from color grading to client-ready delivery.
